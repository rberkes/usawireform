Snapshot 18 Sep 2026. This is not “how do we get bigger than Thomas.” Thomas
already won that game. This is how **usawireform.com** becomes the place a
buyer goes when they have a **wire print**, and the place a shop pays because
the lead actually fits the cell.

## The honest win condition

You do not beat Thomas at 500,000 suppliers, 2.2 million registered buyers, or
a CAD library. You do not beat IQS overnight at encyclopedia SEO they have
been farming since 2000. You do not beat MFG.com at RFQ *volume* across every
process on earth.

You beat them at **one job**: a 3/8 in 3D CNC V-hook, 5,000 pcs, ZIP 44035.

| If the buyer uses… | What they get |
| --- | --- |
| **Thomas** | A category dump. “Wire Forms” shows **723 custom manufacturers**, plus distributors, finishers, and Xometry partners. They pick names. They email. Half cannot run the diameter. |
| **IQS** | A long article, then an RFQ that goes to whoever **paid for the heading**. Match quality = who wrote the check. |
| **MFG.com** | A job on a global board. Shops unlock. Lowest bid often cannot buy the steel in the US. NPS around **−34**. |
| **USA Wire Form (as designed)** | Cell class + diameter hard filter. Same-state shops fill six. Desk holds, then Releases. First two shops pay **$49**. Buyer never hears about shop fees. STEP never on email. |

**Winning** means: when the print is wire, we produce two real quotes from
shops that can form it, cheaper and cleaner than the other three. Not more
traffic than Thomas. Not more categories than IQS. Not more RFQs than MFG.

Xometry is the silent fourth. Instant black-box price, they take the job and
farm it. Our instant estimate is a **rate card on one cell** (214TF, 4–14 mm).
Do not pretend it is Instant Quote Nation. Use it as proof we know the math.

## Who is who

### Thomas (Thomasnet)

The phone book that learned ads. Informa. North American industrial search.

- **For buyers:** free search, filters, shortlists, RFI/RFQ to names *they*
  pick. 6M+ products, 10M CAD models.
- **For shops:** claim free (weak placement). Money is **Verified /
  sponsored / performance-based listings**. PPC on “evaluation clicks.”
  Category programs quoted in the market from a few thousand to **$50k+/yr**.
- **Wire Forms heading (live):** 723 custom manufacturers, 519
  “manufacturers,” 209 service companies, **67 Thomas Verified**, 4 Xometry
  Partner, 92 “Made in USA.” Those buckets overlap and include shops that
  mentioned wire once.
- **How a wire print dies:** capability is a paragraph. No diameter band. No
  cell class. No “this Robomac cannot run 2 mm spring wire.” Buyer broadcasts.
  Shops ignore junk. Paid shops get the click whether they can run it or not.

**Strength:** brand, traffic, procurement habit, cert/diversity filters.
**Kill shot against them:** they sell *discovery*. They do not sell *fit*.
A 723-row list is the problem they call a feature.

### IQS Directory (Industrial Quick Search)

SEO mill with a patented preview ad. Founded ~2000 by a former Thomas rep.

- **For buyers:** category encyclopedia (history, materials, glossary) +
  rollover site previews + RFQ form.
- **For shops:** **flat annual fee**, historically **~$2,200–$4,800** for page-one
  on a heading (older rate cards: ~$2k page 1, $499/$199 page 2). A handful of
  advertisers own the heading. Everyone else is content.
- **Wire forms page:** genuine process copy, then “contact the companies
  listed above.” The list is **who paid**, not who can form 8 mm 3D.
- **Traffic engine:** Google long-tails. They buy/rank keywords, then sell
  the click to five logos.

**Strength:** they already rank for “wire form manufacturers.”
**Kill shot against them:** the RFQ is a **paid-placement blast**. An engineer
who needs fourslide 0.080 in and a shop that paid for “wire forms” and runs
only 3D CNC 10–14 mm both lose. IQS cannot say no to an advertiser.

### MFG.com

Custom-parts marketplace. Closest cousin to Source — and the one shops hate.

- **For buyers:** free to post. One RFQ, many quotes. Compare price / lead.
- **For shops:** profile can be free. To quote: **Professional ~$299/mo**
  pay-per-unlock, or Premium unlimited in selected capabilities. NDA to see
  files, then pay to see the buyer.
- **Coverage:** machining, molding, sheet, 3D print, **springs & wire forming**
  as one line among dozens. Global. China shows up in the bid stack.
- **Reputation:** shop forums say race-to-bottom, material cost not covered,
  win rate near zero. Comparably NPS **−34**, CSAT **33**.

**Strength:** they already trained shops to **pay to unlock a print**.
**Kill shot against them:** they optimized for **quote count**, not **fit**.
A hungry mill in another country can buy the same lead as a fourslide in
Grand Rapids. Our house rule is the opposite: hard filter first, same-state
first, cap of six, first two unlock, waitlist does not pay to sit.

### USA Wire Form (usawireform.com)

Two products on one domain. That is the brand wound and the weapon.

1. **This floor** — Northeast Ohio, Robomac 214TF, 4–14 mm, rate card,
   production quote. A real shop.
2. **Source + directory** — 539 wire plants (517 US, 22 Canada), iron tags
   from public pages, matching on **filed cells**, $49 AI Smart Connect™.

| What we actually have | What we pretend if we get sloppy |
| --- | --- |
| 539 **wire** plants, researched, TSVs in repo | “500k suppliers” |
| Match on cell + mm, then ZIP state | “RFQ to the industry” |
| Desk is the gate | Automation theater |
| $49 when the print fits | $299/mo for a feed of junk |
| Instant estimate = this cell’s math | Xometry |
| Directory ≠ teaser pool until they file | 539 shops get every job |

**Strength:** domain knowledge in `source-iron.ts` (Numalliance, WAFIOS, AIM,
Baird, Nilson, Bihler, Whitelegg). `/find-factories-by-machine`. Honest empty
states. House floor **cannot** buy its own teaser pool.
**Kill shot against us, today:** filings << listings. Clerk is still
`pk_test`. `info@` has **no MX**. NDA off. Brand says manufacturer. IQS/Thomas
still own the generic Google queries.

## How each one actually loses (use this)

**Thomas loses** when the buyer learns the list is a heading, not a capability
index — and when the shop learns they paid for clicks from prints they cannot
run. Counter: **filter by iron, then ZIP, then six.** Public page:
`/find-factories-by-machine` and `/directory?iron=fourslide`.

**IQS loses** when Google starts sending the long-tail to a page that *does
the match* instead of a 3,000-word glossary with five logos. Counter: **landers
that end in a Source form**, not an “email these advertisers” box. Keep the
education (we already have process pages). Put the STEP upload on the same
URL.

**MFG loses** every time a US shop quotes against a 40% below-steel bid.
Counter: **USA filings only, capability hard filter, desk gate, $49 not
$299/mo, waitlist does not pay.** Say it on `/source/shops`. Do not be shy:
“Not a global bid board.”

**We lose** if we:

1. Send teasers from the directory instead of filings (junk leads → we become MFG)
2. Expand to stamping/molding before wire pools fill (we become a thin Thomas)
3. Charge shops rent for placement (we become IQS)
4. Keep `pk_test` and a dead `info@` (we look like a brochure)
5. Let the Ohio floor sit in the pool (we look like a shop in a costume)

## Game plan — beat them at wire, then stop talking about them

Do this in order. Later rounds are illegal until the earlier ones print.

### Round 0 — Don’t die on the table

Without this, there is no fight.

1. MX for `info@usawireform.com` (Zoho/Google on `@`, Resend stays on `send.`)
2. Clerk **production** (`pk_live`, custom `clerk` / `accounts` / `clkmail`, Google OAuth off until you have your own client)
3. Stripe live + webhook
4. Hide Clerk’s logo on the card (`logoPlacement: "none"`); keep the site lockup until a real rebrand
5. `LEADS_NOTIFY_EMAIL` off Gmail

### Round 1 — Own the inventory (this is how you beat Thomas’s 723)

Thomas sells a heading. We need **filed cells**.

6. Spreadsheet: 539 listed → how many filed, by state, by class (spring / 2D /
   3D / straighten-cut / fourslide / multi-slide). That gap **is** the company.
7. Invite **OH, MI, IN, PA, IL, WI** first. Source invite mail already exists.
   Target: enough filings to fill **4 of 6** teasers on a common 4–14 mm 3D print.
8. Turn **NDA on**.
9. Do not ingest more associations. SMI/CASMI are mined. Conversion is the work.

**Beat Thomas when:** a buyer can say “fourslide 6 mm Michigan” and get six
real floors, not 723 logos.

### Round 2 — Own the loop (this is how you beat MFG)

10. One print: hold → Release → shop pays $49 → buyer answers. Screenshot it.
11. Second $49 from the same shop (STRATEGY gate 1). That is the sales deck.
12. Outcome nudge email (shops who never report). Then decide refund vs credit
    — after you have something to refund.
13. Shop one-pager vs MFG: “$0/month. $49 when the cell matches. US. Six shops.
    Desk. No China bid.”

**Beat MFG when:** win rate on paid unlocks is a number you can say out loud
and shops come back.

### Round 3 — Own the query (this is how you beat IQS)

Do not try to out-glossary IQS on “what is a wire form.” Out-convert them.

14. Every high-intent lander (`/fourslide`, `/cnc-wire-forming`, state pages,
    `/find-factories-by-machine`, powder/staple hubs) ends in **Send a print**,
    not a brochure CTA.
15. Title/H1 for machines and diameters: “Robomac shops,” “Bihler multi-slide,”
    “8 mm 3D CNC wire forming,” “fourslide Ohio.” IQS and Thomas are weak here
    because their unit of sale is a **heading**, not a **cell**.
16. Directory pages stay indexed; empty/thin ones already noindex. Substance
    over volume. Ignore the old SEO-expansion PRs.
17. Mark `generate_lead` on contact + Source in GA4. IQS sells “traffic.” We
    sell **matched RFQs**. Measure that.

**Beat IQS when:** for those long-tails, we rank and the click files a job,
not a preview ad.

### Round 4 — Own the story (this is how you don’t lose the brand fight)

18. Public split: **Directory / Source** is the map. **This floor** is one
    plant on it (214TF, 4–14 mm). Homepage already tries; sign-up, mail, and
    `info@` still say job shop.
19. Never tell buyers about shop $49. Never put the house in the six.
20. Shop sales: steal budget from Thomas/IQS renewals. “You already pay them
    four figures to be in a heading that includes Xometry. File the cell free.
    Pay when it fits.”

### Round 5 — Only after the four gates

From STRATEGY.md: (1) shops buy a second lead, (2) buyers answer, (3) released
prints fill ≥4/6, (4) buyers send a second print.

Then, and only then:

- Certs as a hard filter (Thomas’s actual remaining advantage)
- Outcome refund
- A neutral domain (`manufacturingeasy.com` is parked — keep it parked until
  wire prints money)
- Another process (stamping etc.) — that is how you become a bad Thomas
